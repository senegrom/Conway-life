# Minimise a periodic target with no periodic predecessor

## Problem

Find the smallest spatial fundamental domain of a periodic Life configuration that has at least one predecessor but no spatially periodic predecessor.

## Baseline

The 2025 Salo–Törmä construction has a 6210 × 37800 fundamental domain.

## Search directions

- compress circuit gadgets;
- quotient symmetries;
- use smaller uniquely constrained agars;
- search finite tori for fibre signatures inconsistent with periodicity;
- minimise each gadget under SAT equivalence;
- optimise lexicographically by area, maximum dimension and population.

## Verification

An explicit predecessor representation and a proof that no periodic predecessor exists, with independently replayable certificates.
