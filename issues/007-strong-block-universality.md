# Strong block-map universality of Life

## Problem

Life is known to be semiweakly universal as a one-step block map. Determine whether it is strongly universal.

## Meaning

A strong simulation requires a bijection between valid simulated assignments and the selected Life predecessor fibre: no extraneous predecessors.

## Computational programme

1. Implement exact finite-torus predecessor counting.
2. Reproduce the bounded exclusions recorded in the July 2026 secondary audit.
3. Extend area and periodic-point tests with symmetry reduction.
4. Search for a universal bijective gadget family.
5. Alternatively identify a block map whose periodic counts or another invariant cannot occur in a Life fibre.
