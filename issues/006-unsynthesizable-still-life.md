# Improve the unsynthesizable still-life bounds

## Current recorded bound

The first population \(p\) for which not every still life is glider-constructible satisfies \(22 < p \le 154\).

## Two routes

### Lower the upper bound

Search smaller self-forcing subsets/agars and minimise known examples.

### Raise the lower bound

Complete syntheses or prove constructibility for all still lifes of the next population class.

## Verification

A failed synthesis search is not an unsynthesizability proof. Require a self-forcing/unique-predecessor argument or equivalent certificate.
